# CSD17577Q3A DNH body envelope

this STEP is a rectangular clearance envelope made from the maximum body
sizes in TI SLPS515A section 7.1: 3.1 x 3.25 x 0.9 mm. its bottom is at z=0
and its center is x=0, y=0. it is not a manufacturer model and does not show
the leads, drain clip, package draft or pin-1 mark. the footprint courtyard
covers the copper and lead extent separately.

source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/csd17577q3a.pdf
package drawing 4222499/A, december 2015.

created with the project's existing CadQuery runtime. the exported STEP was
reopened and its dimensions, one solid and volume were checked. use this only
for body clearance until a verified manufacturer DNH model is available.
